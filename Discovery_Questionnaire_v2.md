# Earned Impact – Outbound Discovery Questionnaire (v2)

*This helps us understand your organization, the communities you serve, your mission-driven offer, and how appointments move forward once booked — so we can build an outbound campaign that sounds like you, not a marketing agency.*

1. Organization Name
2. Website URL
3. LinkedIn Page
4. Briefly describe your organization and the mission you're working toward.
5. Which program, service, or membership offering should we prioritize in outbound conversations?
6. In one sentence, what does this help the people you serve actually get or achieve?
7. What's the real gap or unmet need this addresses for them?
8. What makes your organization different from others doing similar work?
9. Why should someone take the time to meet with you, rather than say they're not interested?
10. Who are we reaching out to on your behalf? (Titles & decision-makers)
11. Which types of organizations, sizes, and geographic areas should we focus on?
12. Are there any types of organizations, or specific contacts, we should avoid reaching out to?
13. Describe the organization or contact who's the best fit for this — the one you'd want on every call.
14. What's the number one thing weighing on the people you're trying to reach right now — in their own words, the way they'd actually say it?
15. What are the top three challenges they're up against?
16. What happens if this stays unresolved for another year — for them, or for the people they serve?
17. What pushback do you hear most often, and how do you usually respond to it?
18. Please share 2–3 wins, stories, or results you're proud of.
19. Please share links or files for anything you already use in outreach — scripts, one-pagers, decks, or recordings.
20. Who on your team will be taking these appointments?
21. Job Title
22. Email Address
23. Phone Number
24. Calendar Booking Link
25. Briefly walk us through what happens after a meeting is booked.
26. What does a genuinely successful appointment look like to you?
27. Have you run outbound campaigns before? *(Yes / No)*
28. If yes, what worked well?
29. Are there any words, claims, or topics we should avoid — anything that wouldn't sound like you?
30. Is there anything else our outbound team should know before we start reaching out on your behalf?

## Recommended Additional Questions for Kaelene Review

Not added to the questionnaire above — flagged separately per Damon's request, since both close a direct qualification gap called out in the SDR Script Package's qualification checklist and Discovery Style guidance, but neither is currently asked anywhere in the existing form.

1. **Capacity to absorb demand** (placement: after Q13, "Describe the organization or contact who's the best fit...") — Right now, could you actually take on more (more students, appointments, or members) if this worked — or would it outpace what your team can handle?
   *Why: the ICP's own disqualifiers rule out any organization "with no program capacity to absorb new demand" — but nothing in the current form asks this directly.*

2. **Decision-making process** (placement: after Q26, "What does a genuinely successful appointment look like to you?") — Besides you, who else is usually part of deciding whether to move forward?
   *Why: called out explicitly in the Discovery Style guidance as something every question should build toward, but it isn't asked anywhere in the current form.*
